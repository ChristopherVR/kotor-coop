## Distribution boundary (2026-10-24)

Development remains private at `ChristopherVR/kotor-coop-dev`. The separate public
`ChristopherVR/kotor-coop` repository starts from fresh history and contains only
curated information and an original animated overview. It has no player release
yet. Legacy game-derived media and deleted recordings are excluded rather than
copied through a Git mirror. See [PUBLICATION.md](PUBLICATION.md).

The boss now uses the original procedural Cairn Sentinel design and synthesized
nonverbal audio. Prior character assets remain only in private history. The address
database's current upstream MIT notice and exact-byte provenance have been recorded.
Neither change establishes legal clearance for the mod as a whole.

## Open gates before publishing player builds or development source

- **Game/store terms:** qualified legal review of applicable agreements for
  supported stores and jurisdictions, including hooks, executable changes,
  reverse engineering and multiplayer use. Steam's linked
  [KOTOR EULA](https://store.steampowered.com//eula/32370_eula_0) currently shows
  general Disney terms restricting modification, derivatives and reverse
  engineering. That is not affirmative mod permission or a conclusion about GOG.
- **Extracted game media:** `media/trailer/audio/` contains original game music
  and voices; `media/trailer/logo.png` was extracted from a movie. See their
  [provenance](../media/trailer/README.md). Obtain permission or remove/replace
  these from public distribution, including accessible Git history, before
  changing visibility. Review footage and website/trailer distribution too.
  A disclaimer, free access or private hosting does not establish consent.
- **Ownership:** confirm authorization from relevant original contributors and
  employer/contractor rights holders before licensing earlier material. Record
  approvals privately. A new license neither revokes earlier grants nor assigns
  copyrights.
- **Dependencies:** [full collected notices](../third-party/NOTICES.txt) now ship,
  with published-version and content-hash guards. The remaining cshtml, assembly
  and theme notices have been recovered and their sources recorded in the
  [notice inventory](../THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md). Recheck against exact packaged runtime,
  native-library and dependency versions, including embedded grammars. Ship
  required full copyright/license texts. Their terms apply independently;
  project restrictions cannot replace them. See [the inventory](../THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md).
- **License review:** counsel must review the custom license, contributor grant,
  ownership identification, enforceability and consumer-law provisions. These
  documents do not establish legal clearance.

## Platform and statutory limits

Public repositories permit viewing and on-platform forks under
[GitHub Terms, D.5](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/github-terms/github-terms-of-service#5-license-grant-to-other-users).
The project preserves these permissions while restricting independent releases
and commercial reuse within its rights. It cannot guarantee no public forks.
See section D.6 for contributions and [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

Australian law contains conditional computer-program interoperability provisions
and protects certain statutory exceptions
([Copyright Act 1968, sections 47D–47H](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C1968A00063/latest/text)).
No finding has been made that this project satisfies those conditions or the laws
of other jurisdictions. Noncommercial status and anti-piracy notices alone do
not establish legality.

Steam terms and the Australian statutory reference were rechecked 2026-10-24.
Development remains private; the curated public hub is not authorization to publish
uncleared player builds, legacy media or development history.
