# KOTOR Co-op v0.11.0

This is the first numbered release since v0.10.0 (early September). A lot has changed, so this
page says what you will notice, in plain terms. For exactly what has and has not been tested, read
[what works and what doesn't](https://github.com/ChristopherVR/kotor-coop/blob/main/docs/limitations.md).

## What's new for players

- **Your own character, backpack and credits.** The first time you join, the normal character
  creation screen opens and you build your own character. Your items and credits are your own, and
  they come back with you after a disconnect. Quest items are still shared so the story keeps working.
- **Enemies scale with the number of players.** With two or more players, enemies have more health
  and hit harder, and shops stock more. Playing alone changes nothing.
- **Becoming a Jedi on Dantooine.** Whoever talks to Master Dorak picks a path; the other player is
  asked on their own screen to pick theirs. Everyone gets a lightsaber in their own colour.
- **Reconnecting is smoother.** Either player can save and load. The other one rejoins by itself in
  about 10 to 20 seconds. If you drop, you come back into the same world.
- **Minigames.** In a swoop race or the Manaan underwater trip, the other player is held in place
  with a "waiting" line and released when it ends. The turret ambush on the Ebon Hawk works the same way.
- **More of the story is shared.** Cutscenes, the Leviathan capture and interrogation, the
  dark-side temple, Manaan, effects such as stun and fear, healing between players, and rescuing a
  downed partner have all been tried on two real copies of the game.
- **Up to eight players can connect.** Eight copies have travelled and fought a shared enemy together.
- **Typing an address in the game.** Choose **Connect to an address...** at the bottom of the server
  list. A gamepad can type using an on-screen letter grid.
- **Mod compatibility checks.** The mod compares your install with the host's when you connect and
  tells you what differs, instead of failing silently.
- **Linux and Steam Deck.** There is now a Linux download with an installer. It has not been tried
  on real hardware yet.

## Fixes you may have run into

- Fixed a crash in both games after going through the Upper City North door on Taris.
- Fixed a crash on Manaan after repeated rescues in a fight.
- A partner standing near the swoop announcer, or the host during a joiner-led race, no longer gets
  pulled onto the race track.
- On Manaan, the joining player is no longer dragged underwater with the host.
- Fewer cases where a partner shows up invisible, as a stranger, or without clothes after an area change.

## Known problems

- A complete two-player run from start to finish has not been played. Expect rough edges in scenes
  nobody has tried.
- The built-in automatic port opening did not work on the one real home connection it was tried on.
  A free tunnel such as playit.gg is the reliable way to play over the internet.
- If the joining player leads the dark-side temple conversation, the scene does not start. Let the
  host lead it.
- The joining player cannot start the Manaan airlock; it crashed their game when they could.
- Leviathan: the joining player crashed once in three elevator rides to the next deck.
- Joiner-led companion recruiting has not been shown to reach the host.

Downloads are below. Windows: extract the zip into your KOTOR folder or run `KotorCoop.Agent.exe`.
Linux and Steam Deck: extract the tarball and run `install.sh`.
