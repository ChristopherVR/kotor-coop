KOTOR CO-OP — gameplay trailer

00:00–00:14  Original title and crawl over a procedural starfield.
KOTOR CO-OP. An experimental fan-made mod.
A familiar galaxy. A friend beside you. Explore distant worlds.
Face the same enemies. Share the adventure.
Licensed orchestral music accompanies the opening, with quiet captured game ambience.

00:14–00:23  EXPLORE TOGETHER — KORRIBAN
Paired views show two characters moving forward in distinct armor.

00:23–00:40  SHARE THE CONVERSATION — DANTOOINE
One player is already speaking to Belaya. The second selects the same
character and joins. The first chooses a reply. Both views follow Belaya
as she describes her Jedi training and Master Zhar. Only the host game
voice is mixed, so speech is not doubled; the licensed score is ducked beneath it.

00:40–00:53  FIGHT SIDE BY SIDE — DANTOOINE
Both players wield blue and yellow sabers against original kath hounds.
Their initial shared enemy dies naturally; nearby combat continues.

00:53–00:57  Closing title and project website.
christophervr.github.io/kotor-coop
KOTOR 1 required for each player.

Scenes were staged using disposable saves and native development controls.
God mode sustained combat filming. These shots do not demonstrate ordinary
combat balance or full-campaign reliability. See provenance.json for hashes
and per-shot evidence.
